Immanuel Löw, Die Flora der Juden

Vienna and Leipzig, 1924–34, four volumes in five: the standard reference on plants in Jewish literature, in German, with Hebrew, Aramaic, Syriac, Arabic and Greek on every page. This reading edition gives the German text and an English translation of a few pages.

What this is, and what it is not

  • The transcription and the translation are made with Claude Opus 5.5. One page (Bd. I p. 194) was checked by hand; its Syriac words are not yet verified.
  • Abbreviations are expanded from Löw's own list in Bd. IV pp. 731–740 where possible (table).
  • Each page links to the scan: please check it before citing anything.
